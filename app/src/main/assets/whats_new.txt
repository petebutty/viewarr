# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Browsing styles, a rebuilt Music, and quieter notifications
- Settings → Appearance → Browsing style: Viewarr, Cinema (big trailer panel) or Lite
- Music has Your Mix, daily mixes, Because you played… and endless radio
- Notifications are off until you choose them (Settings → Menu & navigation → Notifications)
- Faster start-up, and pages open from what's kept on the box
