# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
A new look: one glass menu, one player bar
- The menu on the left is see-through glass on every page and slides the page aside; moving over Live TV, Movies, Shows, your streaming services (Netflix, Disney+…), Music or Recordings shows that page straight away
- Live TV keeps playing in the top-left window while you browse, with the details of what you're on beside it; with nothing on, trailers play there
- One info bar for live TV and films, with the controls inside it; every pop-up uses the same glass
- Edit menus (top of the menu): show, hide and reorder everything in the menu, a service's rows and your libraries, right there
- Live now and Sports are in the menu; the shortcut row is gone. Remote buttons is now one list of every button, each set per place; and a new Viewarr theme
