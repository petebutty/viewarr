# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
New search, Watch Next and more
- Search pops up over any screen: hold Menu (≡), or pick a button in Settings → Remote & navigation
- Search finds your Plex, Jellyfin and Emby titles, people, channels and what's on TV, with voice search
- From search or the guide: record a programme, or have Viewarr remind you when it starts
- Your Continue watching now shows in the TV home screen's Watch Next row
- Request films and shows on Seerr: hold OK on a poster (connect Seerr in Accounts & sources)
- The TV guide shows a picture of the highlighted programme
