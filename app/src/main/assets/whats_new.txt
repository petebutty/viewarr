# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Trakt sync, Watch from the start and new player controls
- Your Trakt watched history now shows in Viewarr: ticks on everything you've watched, and Play / Resume picks up where you really are. Sync now in Accounts & sources → Trakt
- Missed the start of something in the guide? Hold OK on it and choose Watch from the start to play it from your library or addons
- Films and shows: OK brings up the seek bar and Audio, Subtitles and Media info buttons; Play/Pause on the remote pauses. The Info button shows the synopsis, and so does pausing
- Catch-up channels: press OK, Up to the progress bar, and move back with Left to rewind
