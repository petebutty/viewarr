# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
IPTV watch progress, and rows under the info bar
- IPTV films and shows remember where you stopped: Resume, watched ticks and Continue watching (kept on this box)
- Down from the info bar: the rows you choose, such as Continue watching, Favourite channels or On now (Settings → Appearance → Info bar & controls)
- My Library in the menu, the buffer on the seek bar, and Previous / Next episode buttons you can add to the info bar
