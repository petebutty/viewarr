# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
A new tour, channel browsing and a tidier menu
- A new tour of how Viewarr works, and before setting up, three pages on what you'll need to have ready
- Browse channels in the info bar: set a button to "Next channel in the info bar" and the channel changes only when you stop on one (Playback → Browsing channels)
- The menu: hints that say what Right does, headings on each list, and optional colour edges, tints and an icon column (Appearance → Main menu)
- Home page & menu is one list per type, each row showing where it comes from; rows can be moved
- Music: a spinning vinyl or CD on Now Playing, lyrics, the queue, a sleep timer, song radio, favourites, artist pages with top songs and similar artists, a screensaver and new visualiser styles
- Next episodes show their release date, darker when not out yet; hold Play to pick a source; square guide corners are back; many focus fixes
