# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Make Viewarr yours
- Settings → Appearance: text size, a clock, channel logos and programme pictures, poster labels, and what title pages show (each rating, tabs, logo, trailer)
- Settings → Playback: picture-in-picture size and corner, what Back does, how long controls stay up, seek-bar previews, next-episode countdown, "Still watching?" and a low-memory mode
- From the start: a button next to Resume plays a film or episode from the beginning
- Settings pages: Left goes back to the section you came from
