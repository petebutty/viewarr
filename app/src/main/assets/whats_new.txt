# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Several Live TV sources, and M3U playlists
- Save several Dispatcharr instances, IPTV logins or playlists, name them, and switch from the guide's menu or full screen (Up, Left, Left). The last three switch instantly
- M3U playlists work as a Live TV source; for logins and playlists, Choose groups leaves out groups you never watch
- Catch-up: rewinding to the start then fast-forwarding works, and Watch from the start shows the search when a title is ambiguous
- The TV guide takes less space on the box
