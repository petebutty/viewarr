Profiles, friends and a tidier app
- Profiles: one per person, with an optional PIN; Dispatcharr admins manage users and what each can do in Settings → Users & profiles
- Friends in another house sign in with your server's outside address and their API key; a new install asks "admin or invited?" and shows a short tour
- Guide: hold OK for Start over (catch-up, else your servers or addons), Go to show, or Request on Seerr
- New icon controls for live TV and title pages; Settings → Appearance → Colours & text sets the date and time format
- Menu: recent searches and voice search under Search, clearer Recordings, search on every Music page, and Transparency sliders in Appearance
