# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Your remote, your way, and catch-up search
- Settings → Remote & navigation: a remote picture for each screen and your choice of what Hold Back, Record, the colour buttons and Info do; Record now schedules in one press
- Guide: Back goes full screen and back (always on your channel), hold Back for the channel groups; live TV glides into the guide's preview
- Search finds catch-up: programmes from the last few days you can replay (also in the guide's hold-OK menu); pills bring their results to the top
- Live TV controls: Transparency sliders over the picture, Settings, and Down for your own shortcuts
- Title pages show badges (age rating, 4K / HDR / Dolby Vision, Atmos, Ends at); the built-in player is now the default, with mpv taking over for unusual files
