# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Skip intros, next episode, and Live now
- Skip intro and Next episode buttons (Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, or TheIntroDB / IntroDB for addons and IPTV); the next episode always plays when one ends, with a countdown you set (0–180 s)
- Live TV hub: a Live now shortcut with what's on the sport and event channels, and Move / Refresh / Hide on any shortcut (films too)
- The live TV info bar: glass or solid panels, channel and episode numbers on or off, HD / 4K, 5.1 and frame-rate badges (Appearance → Live TV)
- Zapping no longer gets stuck loading (Channel change delay in Remote buttons → Live TV); IPTV title pages now fill in from TMDB
- Fixes: shows open on the one-page title view, film and live TV shortcut rows match, guide marks only the programme being recorded
