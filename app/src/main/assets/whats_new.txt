# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
The Live TV hub, presets and a smarter menu
- New, optional Live TV hub (Settings → Navigation → Start → Style): opens on your last channel, and OK then Down on live TV shows shortcuts (Netflix, BBC One, your Plex…) that open with the channel carrying on in the corner
- Settings → Remote buttons → Presets: TiviMate-style or Viewarr in one go, then change any button; a new Stop button on live TV
- The menu: Right on a library lists its categories or genres to jump into, the list slides out of the way, and hold OK on a library edits the list
- Settings is clearer: Remote buttons and Navigation are separate, and a line says what each entry covers
- Back on the guide asks to exit again (Classic), Back twice quickly always exits, and several focus fixes
