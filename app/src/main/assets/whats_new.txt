# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Your setup, saved on Dispatcharr
- Viewarr now keeps your setup on your Dispatcharr server: services, home rows, appearance and settings
- A new TV signed in to the same Dispatcharr user offers to restore it, so there's no setup to do again
- Sign-ins are encrypted before they're saved; turn it off or leave them out in Settings
- The TV guide shows the programme's own picture from your guide when it has one
- Hold OK on a recording in the menu to play, delete, stop or cancel it
