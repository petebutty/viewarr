# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Browse your whole library
- Movies and Shows in the menu now list your libraries and catalogues: open a library a page at a time
- In a library: sort, filter by genre or unwatched, and search it (with voice)
- Hide or reorder the menu's libraries in Settings → Home rows
- Several Plex servers on one account are all used now
- Channel list: press Right on a channel for its programmes, earlier ones playable from catch-up
- Seerr requests work for titles from Stremio addons too
