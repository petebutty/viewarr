Picture-in-picture, show pages and library tools
- Back while a film or episode plays shrinks it to a corner; hold Back for full screen again. Live TV stops while it plays
- Show pages list the seasons and episodes, opening where you're up to, and load much faster
- Films: pick Audio & subtitles before Play, and Mark watched. Libraries: A–Z jump, and your sort is remembered
- Voice search works on the Nvidia Shield; the player's buttons are icons, with Stop next to Play
