# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
One Play button, and pause live TV
- Play picks the best copy for you: your home server, then your servers away, then addons, then IPTV. Hold OK on Play to choose one yourself
- Shows have Play / Resume on their page, carrying on where you left off
- Pause and rewind live TV on catch-up channels, then carry on or jump back to live
- IPTV films and series from Dispatcharr as libraries, a shorter Movies / Shows menu, actor pages with biographies, and film collections
