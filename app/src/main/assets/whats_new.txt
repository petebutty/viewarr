# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Sports, next episodes and the film mini player
- Sports in the menu: your teams' games as panels with both badges; OK goes to the channel showing it (your guide, the UK Sport on TV plugin or the event channels)
- Watching a show, press Down for the next episodes; Back from a film or episode keeps it playing top left while you browse (Stop ends it)
- Where it's playing from on the info bar, a Pop-up glass slider, Glass colours, more fonts and softer highlight colours
- Episode numbers can be turned off in the guide; the guide refreshes on a schedule you choose; ratings can be put in your order
- OK on a recorded programme in the guide plays the recording; settings pages make room for the mini player, the guide keeps your channel in the middle, and many fixes
