# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Sports, shortcuts everywhere, and easier browsing
- Sports: follow your teams and leagues (Settings → Sports); the Sports shortcut shows their games, results and which channel shows each, weeks ahead with the UK Sport on TV plugin
- The live TV shortcuts are now always there (no separate hub style): browse a shortcut with live TV in the corner, and it stops as soon as you pick something
- Hold OK on a shortcut to choose the rows under it; in Continue watching, OK carries on and hold OK goes to the show or season
- The picture in the corner: press towards it to highlight it, then Full screen or Close; Settings → Playback → After a film or episode can go back to live TV
- The guide and channel list can jump from the last channel to the first (Appearance → End of a list); smoother shortcuts, search over a playing film, and many fixes
