# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Multi-view, wallpapers and new themes
- Multi-view: watch up to four channels at once. Press OK while watching and choose Multi-view; the sound follows the window you select
- Wallpapers behind the guide and menus (Settings → Appearance → Wallpaper), and Movies and Shows fill the page with the highlighted title's artwork
- Nine more colour themes, including Nord, Dracula, Forest and High contrast
- Fixes: moving between seasons, title pages on slow servers, and cancelling a recording straight from the guide
