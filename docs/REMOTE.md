# Remote control

Viewarr is made for a TV remote. This page lists what the buttons do out of the box, and how to change any of them.

## What the buttons do

These are the **Viewarr** buttons (the default). Where the **Classic** preset differs, it says so.

| Where | Button | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| TV guide | OK | Watch live, or replay a past programme. On a programme still to come: show its details. |
| TV guide | Hold OK | Record options (this programme, or the series), and more |
| TV guide | Left | Back a programme, and on into catch-up (tap or hold). It stops at the start of catch-up. Classic: on what's on now, the channel groups; hold Left for catch-up. |
| TV guide, a channel or film playing | Back | Jumps to now, then back to it full screen (one press with **jump to now first** off) |
| TV guide, nothing playing | Back | Jumps to now, then the menu. Classic: jumps to now, then asks to exit. Change it in Remote buttons → Back: **jump to now first** (On / Off), and **In the TV guide** (ask to exit or open the menu). |
| TV guide, a channel in the window | Hold Back | Close the player |
| TV guide, nothing playing | Hold Back | The menu, on the channel groups (Left again: the main menu) |
| TV guide | Menu (≡) | The main menu |
| TV guide | Play / pause, Info | The programme's details |
| TV guide | Red / Green / Yellow / Blue | Record options / channel groups / a day back / a day forward |
| TV guide | Stop | Stop the channel in the preview |
| TV guide | Number keys | Type a channel number |
| Watching live TV | Back | The mini player: the guide, with the channel in its window |
| Watching live TV | Hold Back | Close the player |
| Watching live TV | Up / Down | Change channel |
| Watching live TV | Left / Right | Rewind ("Rewind?" first) / skip ahead. Classic: channel list / the channel watched before. |
| Watching live TV | Stop | Stop, back to the guide |
| Watching live TV | Channel + / − | Change channel |
| Watching live TV | OK | The info bar with the controls, on Stop (TV guide, stop, audio, subtitles, aspect, source, stream info, record…). In catch-up: Down from the seek bar goes to Go live. |
| Watching live TV | Hold OK, Menu (≡) | The main menu |
| Watching live TV | Hold Play / pause | Back to live |
| Watching live TV | Red / Green / Yellow / Blue | TV guide / channel groups / the controls / Settings |
| Watching live TV | Record | Record what's on, straight away |
| Films & shows | Left at the start of a row, or Back | The menu |
| Film player | OK | The info bar with the controls. Left from the first button: the menu, over the film. |
| Film player | Down, then Left / Right, OK | Seek bar with previews: move, then jump there |
| Film player | Down from the buttons | The rows under the info bar: next episodes (shows) and the rows you choose; Down / Up between them |
| Film player | Back | Close what's open (controls, details), then the mini player: it keeps playing top left while you browse |
| Film player | Hold Back | Close the player (your place is saved) |
| Mini player (live TV or a film) | Back | Full screen |
| Mini player (live TV or a film) | Hold Back | Close the player |
| Menu | Left / Right | Left on an entry: its own list (Left again: the main list). Right or OK: into it. |
| Menu | Back | Close the menu |
| Film player | Stop (or the Stop button) | Stop (your place is saved) |
| Film player | Info | The poster, episode and synopsis |
| Music | Hold Back | Bring Now Playing back |
| Anywhere | Hold Menu (≡) | Search |
| Anywhere | Mic | Search, already listening (with Viewarr chosen as the TV's voice search app) |
| Anywhere, nothing playing | Hold Back | The menu |

Remotes with **Guide** and **Record** buttons open the TV guide and record what's on.

## Changing the buttons

**Settings → Remote buttons**: every button on the remote has its own entry.

### Presets

At the top, a preset sets every button at once. You can then change any you like.

- **Use Viewarr buttons** (how Viewarr comes): the Classic buttons with a few changes. In the guide, Left carries on back into catch-up (no hold), Back jumps to now and then opens the menu, and the colour buttons record, open the groups and move a day. On live TV, Left / Right rewind and skip, and holding Play / pause goes back to live.
- **Use Classic buttons**: the layout most IPTV apps use. Live TV: Up / Down change channel, OK the controls, Left the channel list, Right the channel before, Menu the main menu. The guide: Left on what's on now opens the channel groups (hold Left for catch-up); Back goes back to the player.

Applying a preset sets every button, and puts the **Search button** back to Automatic and removes any **extra buttons** you'd taught, so nothing left over can clash with it.

### Every button, in every part of Viewarr

Below the presets, each button has a section: **OK**, **Long OK**, **Back**, **Long Back**, **Left**, **Long Left**, **Right**, **Long Right**, **Up**, **Long Up**, **Down**, **Long Down**, **Ch +**, **Ch −**, **Menu**, **Long Menu**, **Play / pause**, **Long Play / pause**, **Stop**, **Rewind**, **Fast forward**, **Record**, **Info**, and the colour buttons **Red**, **Green**, **Yellow** and **Blue**.

Each section lists what the button does in each place, with its own choice:

- **In the TV guide**
- **On live TV**
- **In films & shows**
- **In menus & pages** (and **In the menu** for Back)

Only the places where a button can be changed are listed. For example, OK and the arrows always move around the guide and pages.

Some of the actions you can choose:

| Place | Examples |
|---|---|
| In the TV guide | Watch, or details for later · Watch in the preview · Record options · Record (straight away) · Start over · Jump to now · Page up / down the channels · Earlier / Later programmes · Back / Forward a day · Channel groups (menu) · Main menu |
| On live TV | Open the controls · Info banner · Channel list · Next / Previous channel · **Next / Previous channel in the info bar** · The channel watched before · Skip back / forward · Go live · Start over · Audio & subtitles · Record · Multi-view · Transparency · TV guide |
| In films & shows | Open the controls · Time and title · The seek bar · Next episodes row · Skip back / forward · Audio & subtitles · Details · Media info · Switch player |
| Everywhere | Search · Movies · Shows · Recordings · Music · Settings · Exit Viewarr · Nothing |

**Next / Previous channel in the info bar** lets you browse channels while the picture stays put. See [Live TV](LIVE-TV.md#browsing-channels-in-the-info-bar).

**Reset buttons** puts every button back.

### Other buttons

The **Other buttons** section has:

- **Skip size**: how far each skip goes (catch-up, paused live TV, films and recordings).
- **Channel change delay**: how long a channel has to stay chosen before Viewarr asks for its stream, so zapping quickly doesn't use up your provider's connections. No wait, up to 2 seconds.
- **Search button**: the remote's search button or hold Menu (≡), a colour button, the Info button, **A button you choose** (press it next), or off (search from the menu only).
- **Extra buttons**: give one of these an extra button, such as a colour button or one your remote has spare: **Play / pause**, **Skip back**, **Skip forward**, **Stop**, **Info**, **Audio & subtitles**, **Channel up**, **Channel down**, **TV guide** and **Record**. Press OK on one, then press the button you want. OK, Back, the arrows and the number keys can't be used.

### Button hints

A small pill at the bottom of the screen says what the buttons do where you are. Choose **First few times**, **Always** or **Off** in **Settings → Menu & navigation → Menu → Button hints**.

### Navigation

**Settings → Menu & navigation** covers the rest of how you get around: where the app opens, the side menu, and poster rows. See [Settings](SETTINGS.md#menu--navigation).

## Controlling Viewarr from outside

Home automation, a web page on your server, or ADB can change channel, stop, record, search and more with `viewarr://` links, for example:

```sh
adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW -d "viewarr://channel?number=101"
```

Play / pause, rewind, fast-forward and stop also work as ADB key events (`input keyevent KEYCODE_MEDIA_PLAY_PAUSE`…).

Every command, with examples (and one for Home Assistant), is in [Controlling Viewarr from outside](AUTOMATION.md).

## Settings that can't break the remote

Whatever you change, the remote keeps working:

- **Back** and **hold Back** aren't in the table where they work the mini player and close the player.
- **OK** can't be set to do nothing, and it keeps its main job (watching in the guide, the controls on live TV and in films) until Long OK, an arrow or Menu is given that job instead.
- The **Search button** and any **extra buttons** own their keys: those keys' rows show what they do instead of choices that would never run. Play / pause and the channel buttons can't be taken for either.
- An old or out-of-range setting falls back to the standard one.
